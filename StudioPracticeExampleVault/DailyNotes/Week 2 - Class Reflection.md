Date: 8 October 2026
1. Feedback Is More Than an Opinion
Before this workshop, I usually thought giving feedback was simply about sharing my opinions on someone's work.
The DasArts Feedback Method made me realise that feedback can be more than just saying whether something is good or bad. It can also be about asking questions, sharing different perspectives, and helping someone see new possibilities in their work.
At first, I found it a little difficult to follow all the steps. Sometimes the structure felt a bit unnatural, especially when I couldn't express my thoughts freely. But I think it also encouraged me to slow down and think more carefully about what I wanted to say.
2. Thinking Beyond What We See
One thing that stayed with me was the VR project we discussed.
The participants followed a virtual path, but they couldn't predict what they would feel under their feet. I found this contrast between seeing and feeling really interesting.
During the concept reflection, I chose the word "Embodiment" because the experience was not only about what people could see in VR, but also how their bodies experienced the physical world.
It made me wonder how much our physical sensations influence the way we understand a virtual environment.
3. Taking Something Into My Own Practice
This workshop also made me think about my own creative process.
When making interactive work, I sometimes focus more on the idea or how the technology works, rather than how other people might experience it.
I think feedback can help me notice things I may have missed. I also realised that I don't always need to give someone a solution. Sometimes asking a good question can be just as useful.
In the future, I want to be more open to different perspectives and use feedback as a way to explore new ideas, rather than just fixing problems.
